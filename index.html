Fifteen gatekeepers

The bosses and their lairs

Each boss sleeps in a lair of its own, out in the world it belongs to: a petrified grove on Earth, a black hole in Io Orbit, a breach in the sky of the End. Walk in and its awakening plays out as a film of about forty seconds. Every boss then fights in at least two stages, and each new stage opens with its own scene.

What's in it

Built for the long climb

Boss-gated progression AStages

Every GregTech tier and every gated mod stays locked, visible but unusable, until its boss falls. One kill opens it for every player on the server, and the ages open strictly in order.

A quest book that reads like history 1,723

Every chapter has more than a hundred quests. Each one is a single tree: the main line runs left to right to the boss, and the side branches (Hearth and Home, Arms and Armour, Engineering, Logistics, Expedition) grow off it.

Lairs, films and stages 31 stage films

Fifteen hand-built lairs, each with a cinematic awakening: widescreen bars, a film grade, real debris and camera work. Cutscenes never break your blocks.

The Colosseum 5 waves

The final chapter is a boss rush on Earth. Ring the bell in the emperor's box, and every boss in the pack comes back up through the arena's lifts, three at a time.

The backstory film The Story

The pack ships its own film: the story of the Ancient Builders' war and every boss you will face, narrated and scored, played in game.

Worlds past the edge 40+ dimensions

The Moon and the planets of Ad Astra and Planets+, the gate worlds of Stargate Journey, Alex's Caves, the Otherside, and the Shattered Null, a broken dimension of its own.

Getting started

Install

  1. Get a launcher

    Use the CurseForge app or Prism Launcher. The pack is built and tested on Prism.

  2. Install Java 17

    Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge needs Java 17. In Prism, set it under Settings → Java.

  3. Install the pack

    CurseForge app: search for Age of the Singularity and click Install.

    Prism Launcher: choose Add Instance → CurseForge, search for Age of the Singularity, and install it. All the mods download automatically.

    Get it on CurseForge

  4. Give it 8 GB of memory

    Open the instance's Edit → Settings → Java and set the maximum memory to 8192 MB (8 GB), the amount the pack is built with. A PC with 16 GB of RAM is recommended.

  5. Play, and open the quest book

    Start a new world. The first chapter, the Stone Age, begins under the forest canopy with no iron at all.

Questions

FAQ

Is this like GregTech: New Horizons?

It follows the same idea: a long climb through GregTech's voltage tiers, with every mod tied to its place in the progression. It runs on GregTech CEu for Minecraft 1.20.1 rather than GT5 on 1.7.10, and it adds a boss to the end of every age.

Why can I see an item in EMI but not use it?

It belongs to a later age. Items of a locked age stay visible so you can plan, but you can't use, pick up or store them until that age's boss is dead. The tooltip names the age.

Where is the next boss?

Each chapter's boss quest tells you where to look: the lair's name, the world it is in and the kind of land around it, such as Earth's swamps, the floor of the frozen oceans, or Mars. Lairs are large and easy to spot once you are in the right place.

When you get close, the quest book ticks off the lair as found. Walk up to its heart and the boss wakes.

How do I start the Colosseum?

The Colosseum stands on Earth, not far from where the world began. There is one in every world. Climb to the emperor's box and ring the bell there. The games open once the Apocalypse Dragon is dead.

If everyone in the arena falls, the games end after thirty seconds, and you can ring the bell again.

Does it work in multiplayer?

Yes. Ages unlock for the whole server: when anyone kills a boss, the next age opens for everybody. FTB Teams shares quest progress within a team.

Can I use shaders?

Yes, through Oculus. The cutscenes' film look switches itself off while a shader pack is active; the films still play.

I found a bug. What should I send?

Post it in #bug-reports on the Age of the Singularity Discord, one post per bug. Attach logs/latest.log and logs/kubejs/server.log from the instance folder, the chapter and quest name or the boss involved, and what you expected to happen. A crash also leaves a file in crash-reports.

Release notes

Changelog

Public beta November 2026

  • Quest book: 1,723 quests in sixteen chapters plus the side chapter Bunkers and Base Defence. Every chapter is one tree, set in its age of history.
  • Bosses: fifteen lairs, a new awakening film for every boss, a film for every boss stage, and the Chronos Sentinels arriving one at a time.
  • The Colosseum: the final chapter is a five-wave boss rush on Earth, open after the Apocalypse Dragon.
  • Lairs: 55% bigger, spread further apart, and dry inside even under the sea.
  • The Shattered Null: two new flowers, the Starveil Lotus and the Voidmist Rose, with their own potions.
  • Safety: cutscenes never break blocks, and bosses keep Earth's gravity on other planets so they no longer fly off when they spawn.
  • New gates: powered armour opens age by age from HV, ComputerCraft opens at MV, and Create: Air Ducts arrives with the Steam Age.
